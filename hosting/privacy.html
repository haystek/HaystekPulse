Haystek Pulse

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Haystek Pulse is an internal app for Haystek employees. Employees can share suggestions, read company updates and answer a short Weekly Pulse, so the company can listen and improve. This page explains what the app collects and how it is used.

How employees join

  • Employees join with a one-time join code from the Haystek employee portal. Each code works only once.
  • Employees do not give their name, email or phone number to join.
  • The app does not record which employee used which code.
  • After joining, the employee gets a personal code. It is needed only to sign in again on a new phone. Please keep it safe.

How admins sign in

Admins sign in with their Haystek office email and a password. A confirmation email is sent to that address to verify it. Passwords are stored securely by Google Firebase. Nobody at Haystek can see them.

What we collect

  • Suggestions: employees can send a suggestion on any day. Each suggestion has its own chat, where the employee and admins can reply to each other.
  • Weekly Pulse: opens once a week, every Friday. Employees choose Great, Good, Okay or Difficult, and can add a reason.
  • Broadcasts: updates that admins send to everyone.
  • A notification token, so the app can send notifications. For example: a reply to your suggestion, a new update, or the Weekly Pulse is open.

What we do not collect

  • We never ask employees for their name, email or phone number. A name is saved only if the employee chooses “With my name” and types it in.
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • No ads and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We never sell or share your data with anyone outside Haystek.

Your name stays hidden

Admins never see who wrote a suggestion. If an employee chooses “With my name”, admins see only the name the employee types in while sending. When admins share news about a suggestion with everyone, the sender’s name is never shown.

For the Weekly Pulse, admins see only team totals: how many people chose Great, Good, Okay or Difficult. For Okay and Difficult, admins also see the reasons people wrote. Admins never see who gave which answer, unless the employee chooses “With my name” for an Okay or Difficult answer.

Who can see your data

Only Haystek Pulse admins can read suggestions and Pulse results, through the app. Haystek’s technical team can access the system only to keep it running.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers in Mumbai, India. It is sent and stored in encrypted form.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. Your account is then removed.

Suggestions you sent before deleting stay in the app, and admins can still see them exactly as they were sent. If you sent one anonymously, it stays anonymous. If you sent it “With my name”, the name you typed stays with it.

Lost your personal code? If you lose it and change your phone, your old account cannot be recovered, because we do not know who you are. You can join again with a new join code.

Who can use the app

Haystek Pulse is only for Haystek employees aged 18 and above.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
